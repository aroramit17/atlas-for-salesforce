# Atlas interface direction

The side panel is an operational field guide for a Salesforce org. Deep slate establishes a calm fixed header; a colored org mark is the one signature move, tying the active org to its tab favicon. Warm paper keeps long field and query sessions readable. Teal identifies actions and selection; rows, tables, and native controls carry the work. The shell uses a compact horizontal tool navigation to preserve width for data. Dense results can scroll within the panel; critical loading, error, and empty states stay visible at the point of work.

The visual system is intentionally local to Atlas and uses no Salesforce or competitor brand assets.
